**`Section.markdown()` dropped a section's own heading on pre-2010 filings.** Older EDGAR HTML nests the TOC anchor inside the heading it marks, so the slicer started collecting after the heading's block had begun. On Google's FY2004 10-K (0001193125-05-065298) 0 of 18 item sections began with their "ITEM N." heading; all 18 do now. (GH #1369)
