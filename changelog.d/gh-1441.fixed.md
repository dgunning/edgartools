**Bank, insurer, fund, REIT and utility line items no longer standardize as totals.** Deposit components carried `TotalDeposits` and interest-income lines `NetInterestIncome`, so Auburn National's FY2023 deposits summed to $1,792.5M by standard concept against the filed $896.2M. 39 of the issue's 45 entries are remapped to their line concept and 6 removed. Other noninterest expense and income get their own concepts, `OtherNonInterestExpense` and `OtherNonInterestIncome`. (GH #1441)
