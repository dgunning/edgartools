**`MultiFinancials.income_statement()` and `cash_flow_statement()` now accept `include_quarterly`.** The keyword raised `TypeError` although the stitch underneath supported it; with `include_quarterly=True`, Apple's 10-Q 0000320193-23-000077 adds a $81,797M quarter column next to its $293,787M year-to-date revenue. The default stays year-to-date only. (GH #1783)
