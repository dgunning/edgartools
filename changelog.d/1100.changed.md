**`FactQuery.to_dataframe()` now gives `preferred_sign` and `fiscal_year` the same dtype on every query.** They are nullable `Int64`, so an absent value is `pd.NA`, not `NaN`. In 5.x they were inferred per query: on Foot Locker's FY2024 10-K, `preferred_sign` read `float64` on the bare query and `int64` on the income statement. `is_dimensioned` is now `boolean`. Use `pd.isna()`, not `np.isnan()`. (GH #1100, bead edgartools-rsyt.2)
