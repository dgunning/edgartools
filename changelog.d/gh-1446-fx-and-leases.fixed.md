**The IFRS exchange-rate effect on cash is no longer "Net Change in Cash", and ASC 842 lease maturities get commitment concepts.** On Opera's FY2025 20-F the $1,944K exchange-rate row was the only `NetChangeInCash` row; it is now `ForeignExchangeEffectOnCash`. The six ASC 842 lessee operating-lease maturity elements now feed the `OperatingLeaseCommitment*` concepts their ASC 840 predecessors use. (#1446)
