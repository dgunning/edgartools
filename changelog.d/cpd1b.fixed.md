**A BDC's non-accrual rate uses the grand total it tags as a schedule row.** With no balance-sheet total, the schedule's rows were summed with their subtotals; Oxford Square's 2026-03-31 total read $1.30B against its $235.4M "Investments in Securities", so the rate read 0.67% instead of 3.7%. Such a total is now used, with `total_source='identifier_total'`. (bead edgartools-cpd1b)
