**`get_concept()` reads cash interest and income taxes paid.** Four groups, `interest_paid`, `income_taxes_paid` and their `_gross` variants, map one tag each and report `basis` ('net' or 'gross') in the metadata, with no fallback between bases. In the CY2025 frames 4,150 filers tag `InterestPaidNet` and 169 tag only `InterestPaid`, for whom `interest_paid` is None.
