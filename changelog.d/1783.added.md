**`MultiFinancials.income_statement()` accepts `include_quarterly`.** The method only took `view`, so the keyword raised `TypeError` even though the stitched statement already had the option. It is keyword-only and defaults to False, which keeps the year-to-date column. On Apple's 10-Q `0000320193-23-000077` the opt-in adds the filed three-month net sales of $81,797 million beside the nine-month $293,787 million. (GH #1783)
