**`filing.xbrl().standardization.industry` was always `None`, and setting it by hand changed standard concepts.** The hook read a `Filer` attribute that does not exist and could not parse the header's `NAME [code]` SIC, and the `industry_overrides` it would have applied only restated, narrowed or swapped base mappings, so they are removed.
