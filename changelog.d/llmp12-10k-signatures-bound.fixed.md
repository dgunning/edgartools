- **A 10-K's last item no longer runs through the signature block.** Item 16 ("Form 10-K Summary None.") on Apple, Caterpillar, Bank of America and others carried the whole signature table, because the SIGNATURES bound was 10-Q only; a 10-K now ends there too unless financial statements, schedules or an exhibit index follow, which stay in the section rather than in none. 10-K truth-set both-exact rises from 90.9% to 91.8%.
