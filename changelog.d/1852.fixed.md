**Balance-sheet `validate()` compared totals from different dates.** Each operand took its row's first non-null value, so National Presto's Q1 2026 10-Q compared April 5 assets with December 31 liabilities and equity and reported a $31,418,000 imbalance. Every operand now comes from one column, named in `metadata["period"]`, and an equation that column cannot complete is reported as `INCOMPLETE_DATA`. (GH #1852)
