**`Document.to_markdown()` let a literal `|` or `\` in a table cell break the table.** Only grouped headers were escaped, so a pipe in a flat header or a body cell opened an extra column; AbbVie's FY2024 10-K page footers ("1 | 2024 Form 10-K") rendered three cells for two. Every pipe-table cell is now escaped. (GH #1483)
