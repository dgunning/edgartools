**`EntityFacts` getters read IFRS filers' own totals.** `get_shareholders_equity()` and `get_operating_income()` now read the ifrs-full totals, and `get_net_income()` returns profit attributable to owners. Of 16 IFRS filers measured, 14 got None for equity and the other two (Toyota, Sony) got their last US-GAAP figure, years old; BHP's net income was 24% high. `get_total_assets()` no longer returns current assets.
