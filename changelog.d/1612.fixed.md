**`PortfolioInvestments.from_statement()` disagreed with `from_xbrl()` on the same filing.** It re-parsed the statement's labels without member candidates or `from_xbrl`'s exclusions, so on 38 BDC 10-K and 10-Q filings it returned no rows on 11 (Hercules, Sixth Street, Crescent among them) and differed in rows or names on the other 27. It now reads the statement's filing through `from_xbrl`. (GH #1612)
