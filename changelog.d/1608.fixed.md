**`download_filings(filings=...)` downloads only the requested filings.** It used to download SEC's daily feed file for every business day between the first and last filing date, 1,298 of them for Snowflake's six 10-Ks. Each filing is now fetched on its own (17.8 MB for Snowflake's 2026 10-K, against 2.9 GB for its day's feed file); `source="feed"` keeps feed files, downloading only the days that hold a requested filing.
