**`aggregate()` summed the filed strings and raised `TypeError` on ordinary numeric facts.** It collected `fact['value']`, which is the lexical value as tagged (`'298085000000'`), and handed that to `sum()`. The parsed number was already on the same row in `numeric_value`, which is what it now aggregates; Apple's FY2023 product revenue by `srt:ProductOrServiceAxis` returns totals instead of failing. A fact with no `numeric_value` is not a number and is skipped rather than poisoning its group. (GH #1277, bead edgartools-n93w.3)
