**`EDGAR_ACCESS_MODE` and the `NORMAL`/`CAUTION`/`CRAWL` modes are deprecated and have no effect.** They advertised a timeout, connection-limit and retry policy that was never wired into the HTTP client; nothing in the package read `edgar_mode`, so all 3 modes behaved identically. Use `EDGAR_RATE_LIMIT_PER_SEC` and `EDGAR_HTTP_TIMEOUT`. The names still import and now warn; removed in 6.0. (GH #1326)
