**`parse_inline_xbrl_set()` resolves inline XBRL facts across a filing's documents.** An exhibit without its own `ix:header`, like IBM's FY2024 inline EX-13, now resolves contexts and units the primary document defines: all 3,983 of its facts get a context, where `parse_html()` on the exhibit alone resolves none.
