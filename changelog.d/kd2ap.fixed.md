**`configure_http(proxy=...)` no longer bypasses the rate limiter and the HTTP cache.** The proxy was passed as a client option, and httpx2 routed every request through its own proxy transport in front of both, so the documented corporate-proxy setup sent unthrottled requests to SEC. The proxy now sits under the cache and rate limiter, and `get_http_config()["proxy"]` also reports one set by `HTTPS_PROXY`. (edgartools-kd2ap)
