**`CurrencyConverter` found no exchange rates in filings whose rate unit has an opaque ID.** Units were matched by substrings of their ID, so SAP's `Unit_Divide_USD_EUR_...` facts were all skipped and `exchange_rates` was empty with no warning. Units are now read from their numerator and denominator, either way round: SAP's FY2025 20-F (`0001104659-26-020058`) gives a 2025 average of 1.1293 USD per EUR, so EUR 1,000 converts to USD 1,129.30.
