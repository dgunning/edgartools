**`to_dataframe(*columns)` no longer builds the full fact width before projecting.** Selecting columns now narrows the frame at construction instead of at the end, so a two-column call on the JPM 10-K fixture drops from 7.59 MiB to 0.71 MiB peak and from 78 ms to 8 ms, with a byte-identical frame.
