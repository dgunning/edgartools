**Statements no longer swap a foreign-currency amount for the reporting-currency one.** Duplicates in different units were ranked on decimals alone, so Disney's FY2025 10-K showed its Canadian debt as CAD 1,300,000,000 instead of USD 900,000,000. Statements now keep the filing's most-used currency, within one context and across contexts for the same period, and log one warning per filing naming the dropped units. All amounts stay in `xbrl.facts`. (GH #1704)
