**Statement rows took their calculation `weight` and `calculation_parent` from an unrelated role.** When the statement's own calculation network had no arc for a concept, the first arc in any role was used, so Sonoco's 10-Q (0000091767-26-000022) gave the income statement's basic share count weight 1.0 under diluted shares from the EPS note. Rows and Facts API facts assigned to a statement now read only that role; the share count's weight is None. (GH #1702)
