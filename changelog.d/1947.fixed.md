**A note's detail tables went missing when the filer left "(Details)" off their titles.** SEC's FilingSummary.xml then files them under Notes, Cover or Uncategorized, so Costco's FY2021 `tenk.notes["Leases"].details` was empty and one lease table was typed as a cover page. A disclosure role with value line items and no text block is now read as a detail; FY2021 Leases has its four tables. (GH #1947)
