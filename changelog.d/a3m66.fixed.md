**`extract_nonaccrual()` labelled a result with another period than the filing's.** With no investment facts at the period, it fell back to other dates in the filing: Ares Capital's cover-page 10-K/A for 2025-12-31 came back as 2025-06-30, its public-float date. A period given, or the filing's own period, is now read as given, and a filing with no data there returns no rate. (bead edgartools-a3m66)
