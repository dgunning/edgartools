**`PortfolioInvestments.from_xbrl()` kept subtotals that repeat their one holding.** Since #1506 a costed row over a single extending row was always kept, so PhenixFIN's company subtotals counted as holdings (+$289.5M on its Q2 2026 10-Q) and New Mountain's FY2025 10-K counted ACI Parent's preferred twice. Such a row is now excluded when the row under it states the same cost, unless that row adds only a counter. (GH #1583)
