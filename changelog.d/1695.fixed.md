**TTM no longer sums quarterly per-share earnings, including `get_ttm("EarningsPerShare...")`.** AMC's 2017 TTM continuing-operations basic EPS was -3.75, the sum of its quarters, against the filed -3.80; a fiscal-year window now returns the filed annual amount (Citigroup FY2024: 6.03, with no Q4 quarter), and other windows raise `ValidationError`. Dividends per share still sum. (GH #1695)
