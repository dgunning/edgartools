**`xbrl.facts` gave income-statement facts the preferred label and sign of a disclosure role.** The Facts API took a concept's preferred label from the first presentation role that had one, so Omega Healthcare's 10-Q (0000888491-26-000024) reported interest expense with `preferred_sign` -1 from a segments note's negated label. Facts assigned to a statement now read that statement's arc, giving no preferred sign, as `get_statement()` does. (GH #1677)
