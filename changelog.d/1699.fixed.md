**`get_ttm()` period labels now use each quarter's own fiscal year.** Apple's TTM revenue to 2023-07-01 was labelled [(2022, 'Q4'), (2024, 'Q1'), (2024, 'Q2'), (2024, 'Q3')] because re-filed comparatives carry the later filing's fiscal year; it now reads 2022, 2023, 2023, 2023. A 52/53-week year closing early next month keeps its year (Costco's quarter to 2023-09-03 was FY2024, now FY2023). (GH #1699)
