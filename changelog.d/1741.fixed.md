**Stitched DataFrame columns could not say how long a period was.** A stitched 10-Q keyed three-, six- and nine-month columns by bare end date, so Apple's nine-month net sales of $293,787M sat under `2023-07-01`. `to_dataframe(period_columns="interval")` now names each column by its exact dates (`2022-09-25 to 2023-07-01`); the default names are unchanged.
