**The multi-period guide called stitched 10-Q statements quarterly.** It now says each 10-Q contributes its year-to-date column, keyed by bare end date, and documents `include_quarterly=True` for three-month columns. On Apple's 10-Q `0000320193-23-000077` the default `2023-07-01` column is nine-month net sales of $293,787 million; the quarter is $81,797 million. (GH #1741)
