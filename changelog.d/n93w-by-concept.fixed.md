**`by_concept()` could not match a concept whose name contains an underscore.** Every `_` was rewritten to `:` before matching, so that the element-id spelling `us-gaap_Revenues` would find `us-gaap:Revenues` — but a local name may carry a literal underscore of its own, and rewriting it produced a second colon that matches nothing. YUM files `yum:YUM_LesseeOperatingLeaseLeaseNotYetCommenced…` at USD 90,000,000 and an exact query for it, in either spelling, returned zero rows; the regex branch was rewritten too. The pattern is no longer altered — it is matched against the concept written both ways. Across 15 filings and 20,094 exact concept queries, no query lost a row and the YUM concept is found in both spellings. (GH #1276, bead edgartools-n93w.4)
