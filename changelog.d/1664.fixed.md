**`get_operating_income()` returned pretax income from continuing operations when the filer reports no operating income.** IBM's FY2024 10-K returned $5.797B ("Income from continuing operations before income taxes") and now returns None, as documented. The label fallback no longer matches pretax, continuing-operations, "Other operating income" or "Non-operating income" rows, so such filers now get None. (GH #1664)
