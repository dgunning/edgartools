- **`Financials.get_revenue()` returns a bank's total net revenue, not one part of it.** Banks tag their top line as `RevenuesNetOfInterestExpense`, which was never searched, so JPMorgan's FY2025 revenue came back as $87.0B noninterest revenue instead of $182.4B, Wells Fargo's as $16.1B fee income and American Express's as $37.4B. A bank with no total row now returns `None` rather than a component.
