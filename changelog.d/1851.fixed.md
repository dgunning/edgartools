**EntityFacts `pivot_by_period()` dropped 13-week quarters of 52/53-week filers.** Period labels counted the calendar months a period touched, so Apple's Q2 and Q3 FY2025 were both `6M 2025` and Q3 net income of $23,434,000,000 (10-Q `0000320193-25-000073`) vanished. Durations are now classified by days, and two periods that still share a label get separate dated columns. (GH #1851)
