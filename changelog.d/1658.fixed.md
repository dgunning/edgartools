**`EntityFacts.time_series("Assets")` returned rows from other concepts.** A bare name was matched by substring, so 18 of the 20 rows for Snowflake's `Assets` came from concepts such as `AssetsCurrent` and `AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets`. A bare name or label now selects exactly that concept, as `get_fact()` does; text that names no concept still falls back to a substring search, with a warning naming the concepts it mixed.
