**`include_dimensions` is removed from statement rendering.** The `Financials` and `xbrl.statements` statement methods, `Statement.render()` and `Statement.to_dataframe()` warned about it since 5.8.0 and now raise `TypeError`; use `view='detailed'` for `True` and `view='standard'` for `False`. `CurrentPeriodView` and stitched statements keep theirs.
