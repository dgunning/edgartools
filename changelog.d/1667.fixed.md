**`get_total_assets()` returned the current-assets subtotal when the total row was not labeled "Total assets".** NovaGold's 10-Q for 2026-08-31 returned $349.8M instead of the filed $573.3M, which threw off `debt_to_assets` too. The getter now reads the `us-gaap:Assets` concept first, as `get_total_liabilities()` does, and its label fallback no longer matches subtotals. (GH #1667)
