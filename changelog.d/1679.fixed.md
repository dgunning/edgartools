**SAP's cash-flow "Dividends paid" row showed the equity-statement total.** `ifrs-full:DividendsPaid` was classed as a cash flow, so SAP's FY2025 row read EUR 2,746M instead of the filed EUR 2,743M. The row now carries the parent-shareholder cash line, dividends to non-controlling interests get their own row, and the equity total stays out of the cash-flow view. (GH #1679)
