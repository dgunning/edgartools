- **A utility's total operating revenue is `Revenue`, not "Electric Utility Revenue".** `RegulatedAndUnregulatedOperatingRevenue` is total operating revenue across electric, gas and water operations; it now maps to `Revenue` and is a listed member of that total. MGE Energy's Q2 2026 Total Operating Revenues of $161,195K, which includes $29,601K of gas revenue, was `ElectricUtilityRevenue` and is now `Revenue`. (#1844)
