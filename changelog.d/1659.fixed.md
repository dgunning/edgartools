**`get_stockholders_equity()` returned the liabilities-and-equity total, or None, instead of stockholders' equity.** American Airlines' FY2025 10-K returned $61.774B, its total assets, for a $3.727B deficit. The getter now reads equity attributable to the parent (US GAAP and IFRS) before total equity, so filers with a noncontrolling interest get a smaller figure than before: Shell's FY2025 is $174.392B, not $175.319B.
