**`get_stockholders_equity()` returned the liabilities-and-equity total, or None, instead of stockholders' equity.** American Airlines' FY2025 10-K returned $61.774B, its total assets, for a $3.727B deficit. The getter now reads `us-gaap:StockholdersEquity` first, then the total including noncontrolling interest, and its label fallback rejects captions that name liabilities. (GH #1659)
