**`CurrencyConverter` took exchange rates from other currencies and hedge contracts.** It read every rate fact without its dimensions and kept the last one per year, so GSK's FY2025 20-F (`0001131399-26-000004`) gave a 2025 average of 1.19 (a EUR swap) instead of the filed US$/£ 1.31. Only target-currency facts off hedge axes are used now, and conflicting rates for a year are reported in `extraction_warnings` instead of guessed. (GH #1650)
