**`FundReport.investment_data()` dropped a holding's other unique identifier.** `Identifiers.from_xml` parsed N-PORT item C.1.e.iv into `identifiers.other`, but no column carried it. On BHK's NPORT-P (`0001410368-26-085673`) 121 of 1,487 securities state one, and it is the only field that tells apart 45 Royal Bank of Canada reverse repos with the same name and title. New columns `other_id` and `other_id_type` carry it as filed. (GH #1586)
