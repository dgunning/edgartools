**N-PORT `SecurityLending` read every "Yes" securities-lending answer as `None`.** A Yes is stated as attributes of `cashCollateralCondition`, `nonCashCollateralCondition` or `loanByFundCondition`, which `from_xml` never read. On SGOV's NPORT-P (`0002071691-26-016719`) the $441,347,500 cash-collateral Yes and two loans by the fund read `None`. They now read `"Y"`, with the amounts in `cash_collateral_value`, `non_cash_collateral_value` and `loan_value`. (GH #1585)
