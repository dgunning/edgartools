**`filing.xbrl()` on a large annual or quarterly report downloads only its XBRL files.** Above 20 MB it fetched the whole submission, so VALE's FY2025 20-F pulled 101.4 MB over the wire for 0.76 MB of XBRL; it now fetches the index page, the XBRL files and `FilingSummary.xml`, with identical statements. Set `EDGAR_SELECTIVE_ATTACHMENT_DOWNLOAD=false` to restore the full download.
