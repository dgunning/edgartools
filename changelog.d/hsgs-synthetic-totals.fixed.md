**Synthetic totals added a statement's subtotals to their own components.** When a concept has only dimensional facts, `_compute_synthetic_total()` grouped them by axis and summed the largest group, but sharing an axis does not make members disjoint: a statement of shareholders' equity carries the components *and* the filer's own subtotals on one axis. Disney's beginning equity balance was reported as $292,766,000,000 against a filed $98,879,000,000, having added the four components, their `ParentMember` subtotal, the noncontrolling interest and the issuer's total member together. The filing's own total member is now preferred, identified by agreeing with the components where there are any; only a genuinely disjoint breakdown is summed, which is the case the helper was added for (#646). The same double count was inflating three more lines of that statement — dividends $4,098M to a filed $1,366M, share-based stock issuance $3,600M to $1,200M, and a pension asset allocation reading 2.0 where an allocation cannot exceed 1.0. Twenty values change corpus-wide and none is lost. (GH #1281, bead edgartools-hsgs.2)
