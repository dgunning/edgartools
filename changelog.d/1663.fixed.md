**`get_net_income()` could return income from continuing operations as net income when its concept lookup missed.** The label fallback took a row such as "Net income (loss) from continuing operations" above the bottom line; on the IBM FY2024 fixture with its bottom line moved to an unmapped concept it returned $6,015M instead of $6,023M. Continuing, discontinued and noncontrolling rows are now excluded from the fallback. (GH #1663)
