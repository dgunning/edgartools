**`filing.accepted_at` gives the exact acceptance time; the submissions-JSON value could be hours off.** `get_filings()` read Apple's 10-Q 0000320193-26-000020 as 14:01:02 UTC for weeks, against a true 10:01:02. `accepted_at` reads the filing's 3-20 KB index header as a tz-aware `America/New_York` datetime, and `filings.accepted_at()` reads a whole set.
