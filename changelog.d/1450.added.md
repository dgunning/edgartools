**Standard concepts for broker, insurer, BDC and utility line items.** Ten industry measures that had no standard concept now have their own: net revenue after interest expense, investment banking revenue, trading gains, securities borrowed, resale-agreement assets, future policy benefits, policyholder funds, paid-in-kind interest income, and regulated and unregulated operating revenue. JPMorgan's FY2023 total net revenue of 158,104M now reads as `NetRevenueAfterInterestExpense`.
