**`get_financial_metrics(include_periods=True)` reports the dates behind each metric.** On a 10-Q, income covers the quarter while cash flow is year-to-date: Apple's Q3 FY2023 revenue is for 2023-04-02 to 2023-07-01, its free cash flow for 2022-09-25 to 2023-07-01. The keyword-only option adds a `periods` mapping; the default result is unchanged. (GH #1656)
