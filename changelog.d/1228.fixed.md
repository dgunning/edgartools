**Cash-flow instant balances reported no unit and no `point_in_time`.** Both lookups walked only the displayed columns, which are durations on a cash flow statement, so Apple's beginning and ending cash rows returned `point_in_time=None` and `unit=NaN` despite their metadata saying `instant` and `usd`. Period type and unit belong to the concept and are now read that way. (GH #1228)
