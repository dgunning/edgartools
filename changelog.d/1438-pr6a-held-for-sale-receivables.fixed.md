**`ReverseIndex.lookup()` standardized held-for-sale assets, contract assets and restricted cash equivalents as "Other" assets.** 48 balance-sheet elements map to `AssetsHeldForSale`, `ContractAssets`, restricted cash, or the fitting receivable, allowance or other-asset concept; a pledged-collateral element is unmapped. On IBM's FY2024 10-K (`0000051143-25-000015`), $900M of financing receivables held for sale was "Other Non-Operating Current Assets" and is now `AssetsHeldForSale`. (GH #1438)
