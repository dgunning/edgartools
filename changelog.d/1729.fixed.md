**`get_financial_metrics()['debt_to_assets']` had no definition.** It is total liabilities over total assets (Apple FY2023: 290,437 / 352,583 = 0.82), not borrowings over assets (0.32), which `FormC.debt_to_asset_ratio` uses under a similar name. The docstring and the extraction guide now say so; the value is unchanged. (GH #1729)
