**`CurrencyConverter` converted in the wrong direction, or 100x off, depending on how a filer quotes its rate.** `to_usd()` always divided by the rate, so GSK's filed US$/£ 1.31 (20-F `0001131399-26-000004`) turned GBP 100 into USD 76.34 instead of 131. Each rate is now read the only way that puts it in a plausible band for its currency. A rate that could read either way, with nothing in the filing to decide, gives None and a warning; `rate_basis` says how each rate was read. (GH #1651)
