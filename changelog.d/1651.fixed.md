**`CurrencyConverter` converted the wrong way for rates quoted as USD per home unit.** `to_usd()` always divided by the rate, so GSK's filed US$/£ 1.31 (20-F `0001131399-26-000004`) turned GBP 100 into USD 76.34 instead of 131. The converter now records each filing's quote direction; `get_rate()` still returns the filed rate, and a pure-number rate's assumed direction is stated in `extraction_warnings`. (GH #1651)
