**Balance-sheet facts now have a `fiscal_period`.** `xbrl().facts` left it empty on every instant fact, so `get_facts_by_fiscal_period()` returned no balance-sheet values. A fiscal year end is now `FY` and another quarter end is that quarter; Microsoft's Q1 FY2027 10-Q query returns 701 facts instead of 290. Dates that close no period stay empty.
