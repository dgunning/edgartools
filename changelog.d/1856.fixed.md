- **Gross PP&E and accumulated depreciation are no longer "Property, Plant and Equipment" (net).** The property-plus-finance-lease, excluding-lessor-asset and owned PP&E elements now map gross balances to `GrossPropertyPlantEquipment` and accumulated depreciation to `AccumulatedDepreciation`. American Airlines' FY2025 balance sheet had three net-PP&E rows ($57,732M cost, $(25,192)M accumulated, $32,540M net); only the $32,540M row is net now. (#1856)
