**`Schedule14D9` for SC 14D-9 tender-offer recommendations.** `filing.obj()` on a target board's solicitation/recommendation statement returns the board's position as `accept`, `reject`, `neutral` or `None` when the language is genuinely hedged, alongside the Item 4 text it was read from. Classified correctly on 20 of 25 SC 14D-9 originals filed in 2025. An amendment that doesn't restate Item 4 (174 of 229 2025 filings) returns `None` rather than raising.
