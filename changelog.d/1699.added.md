**`get_ttm()`, `get_ttm_revenue()` and `get_ttm_net_income()` take a `filed_by` cutoff.** Only facts from filings made on or before that date are used, before any quarter is derived, and only a gap-free window qualifies: Apple's TTM revenue with `filed_by="2023-08-03"` is $385.1B to 2023-04-01, because the Q3 10-Q came a day later. `as_of` is unchanged.
