**`OwnershipComparison.is_liquidating` reported a sale against a Schedule 13D/G filing that gives no share figures.** A 13D/A or 13G/A may omit them and a pre-2025 header-only filing has none, but they were read as 0: an amendment omitting them on the Aadi Bioscience 13D in the test data sold all 4,535,000 shares. The direction flags are now False when the change is unknown, and `shares_change` and `percent_change` warn that they return None in 6.0. (GH #1379)
