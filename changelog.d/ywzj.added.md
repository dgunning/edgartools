- **`report.section(item)` says how reliably an item was located**: it returns the `Section` that `report[item]` reads, with `.confidence`, `.warnings` and `.detection_method`; `strict=True` raises the new `LowConfidenceSectionError` instead of returning a doubtful one. On 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 20-F and 40-F. NVIDIA's FY2025 Item 8, a 207-character pointer to Item 15, reports confidence 0.5 with an incorporation-by-reference warning.
