**Inline XBRL `format` names now resolve in the registry their prefix is declared for.** A format matched its local name under any namespace, so TR3's nonexistent `fixed-zero` counted as known, and TR4's `date-month-year`/`date-year-month`, TR5's `num-*-decimal-apos` and five `ixt-sec` rules were missing; `ixt:date-month-year` over `12/2025` now gives `2025-12`. Non-English date rules remain unimplemented. (GH #1743)
