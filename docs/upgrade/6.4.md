# Upgrading to EdgarTools 6.4

6.4 keeps every 6.3 name and signature. This page lists what can alter what your
code receives, in the same form as the [6.1](6.1.md) and [6.0](6.0.md) guides:
what a call did in 6.3.0, what it does now, and a filing where the difference
was measured. Changes from 6.1.0 to 6.3.0 are in [Upgrading to 6.1](6.1.md).

If you find a difference this page does not describe, it is a gap in the page;
see [Getting help](6.0.md#getting-help).

---

## Filings: a successor registrant includes its predecessor's

When a company forms a holding company or redomiciles, SEC
gives its ticker to a new CIK and leaves the earlier filings under the old one.
For the 10 successions reviewed by hand, `get_filings()` now includes the
predecessor's history, as `get_facts()` already did since 6.0, so `latest_tenk`,
`get_financials()` and `proxy_season()` read it too (GH #2034).

| Call | 6.3.0 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `Company("XOM").get_filings(form="10-K", amendments=False)` | 0 rows | 31 rows on 2026-11-23, Exxon Mobil Corp's 10-Ks through FY2025 (`0000034088-26-000045`) |
| `Company("XOM").get_financials()` | `None` with a `PredecessorHistoryWarning` | the FY2025 statements |
| columns of a successor's filings | the 13 SEC submissions fields | also `cik` and `company`, naming each row's filer; `filing.cik` is `34088` for a predecessor row |
| everyone else's filings | | unchanged |

A predecessor contributes what it filed before the succession, plus 10-K, 10-Q,
20-F and 40-F reports for periods that ended before it; a filing made under
both CIKs is listed once, as the successor's. Pass `predecessors=False` for the
new CIK's own filings, and use `Company(34088).successor` for the reverse.
