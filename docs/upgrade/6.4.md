# Upgrading to EdgarTools 6.4

6.4 keeps every 6.3 name and signature. This page lists what can alter what your
code receives, in the same form as the [6.1](6.1.md) and [6.0](6.0.md) guides:
what a call did in 6.3.0, what it does now, and a filing where the difference
was measured. Changes from 6.1.0 to 6.3.0 are in [Upgrading to 6.1](6.1.md).

If you find a difference this page does not describe, it is a gap in the page;
see [Getting help](6.0.md#getting-help).

---

## Filings: a successor registrant includes its predecessor's

When a company forms a holding company or redomiciles, SEC
gives its ticker to a new CIK and leaves the earlier filings under the old one.
For the 10 successions reviewed by hand, `get_filings()` now includes the
predecessor's history, as `get_facts()` already did since 6.0, so `latest_tenk`,
`get_financials()` and `proxy_season()` read it too (GH #2034).

| Call | 6.3.0 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `Company("XOM").get_filings(form="10-K", amendments=False)` | 0 rows | 31 rows on 2026-11-23, Exxon Mobil Corp's 10-Ks through FY2025 (`0000034088-26-000045`) |
| `Company("XOM").get_financials()` | `None` with a `PredecessorHistoryWarning` | the FY2025 statements |
| columns of a successor's filings | the 13 SEC submissions fields | also `cik` and `company`, naming each row's filer; `filing.cik` is `34088` for a predecessor row |
| everyone else's filings | | unchanged |

A predecessor contributes what it filed before the succession, plus 10-K, 10-Q,
20-F and 40-F reports for periods that ended before it; a filing made under
both CIKs is listed once, as the successor's. Pass `predecessors=False` for the
new CIK's own filings, and use `Company(34088).successor` for the reverse.

## Equality: `Company` objects compare by CIK

Two `Company` objects were equal only if they were the same object. They are now
equal when they have the same CIK, so they de-duplicate in a set and work as
dictionary keys. This applies to `Entity` and fund entities too.

| Expression | 6.3.0 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `Company("AAPL") == Company(320193)` | `False` | `True` |
| `len({Company("AAPL"), Company("0000320193")})` | `2` | `1` |
| `Entity(320193) == Company(320193)` | `False` | `True` |
| `Company("XOM") == Company(34088)` | `False` | `False`: a successor and its predecessor are different registrants |
| `Company(320193) == 320193` | `False` | `False`: compare `company.cik` |

If your code relied on two objects for one company being unequal, for example
counting `Company` objects in a set, compare with `is` instead.

## Values that change

### 6-K reports

| Call | 6.3.0 | Now | Filing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SixK.exhibits` | every attachment except graphics and the cover page, including the XBRL instance, linkbases, `R*.htm` viewer pages, `Show.js`, `report.css` and `Financial_Report.xlsx` | the filing's `EX-*` documents, the rule `filing.exhibits` applies | Futu 6-K 0001104659-22-129316: about 140 attachments → EX-99.1, EX-99.2 |
| `SixK.text()` | the text of every entry in `exhibits`, without the cover page | the cover page and the `EX-*` exhibits, as `EightK.text()` renders | Futu 6-K 0001104659-22-129316: 13,528,421 characters → 665,948; Cresud 6-K 0001654954-18-006914: empty → the cover page |

### Company facts (`get_facts()`)

These values are derived when a filer does not tag the total itself. A derived
value now comes from one reporting period; where no single period supplies
every part, the call returns `None` rather than mixing periods.

| Call | 6.3.0 | Now | Filing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `get_gross_profit()` from revenue less cost, `annual=True` | each part was the newest fact of any length, so annual gross profit could be one quarter | the latest fiscal year; `annual=False` gives the latest quarter | Procter & Gamble FY2026 10-K 0000080424-26-000103: $11,598M (Q1 FY2027) → $43,670M |
| `get_gross_profit()` and `get_revenue()` from components whose periods differ | the parts were combined anyway | `None`; an older year is not used in place of the latest | Starbucks: $33.68B (FY2026 revenue less a 2020 half-year cost) → `None` (GH #1979) |
| `get_concept("operating_cash_flow")` when only a continuing-operations subtotal is tagged and the same period has nonzero discontinued operating cash | the continuing subtotal | `None`; a filed total, or a subtotal with no discontinued cash, is still returned | SIFCO FY2025 10-K 0001628280-25-058405: $127,000 → `None` (GH #1987) |

|---|---|---|---|

### 10-K and 10-Q sections

| Call | 6.3.0 | Now | Filing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tenk["Item 7"]` and other items in filings laid out as absolutely positioned lines | a line holding an empty spacer `div` was read as having no text, so sections could stop mid-sentence or open past their heading | the line is read as text; over 17 filings in this layout, 20 sections now open on their own heading and 55 have a corrected end | Henry Schein FY2023 10-K 0001000228-24-000011: Item 7 ended at "please see" → ends at "included under Item 8." |
