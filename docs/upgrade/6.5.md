# Upgrading to EdgarTools 6.5

6.5 keeps every 6.4 name and signature. This page lists what can alter what your
code receives, in the same form as the [6.4](6.4.md) guide: what a call did in
6.4.0, what it does now, and a filing where the difference was measured. Changes
from 6.3.0 to 6.4.0 are in [Upgrading to 6.4](6.4.md).

If you find a difference this page does not describe, it is a gap in the page;
see [Getting help](6.0.md#getting-help).

## Values that change

### Statements and XBRL

| Call | 6.4.0 | Now | Filing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xbrl.current_period.statement_of_equity()` | filtered to the period-end instant: the opening balance showed the closing one, and no flow rows (net income, dividends, buybacks) | the fiscal year's duration (the quarter in a 10-Q), with the opening balance from the prior period end | Apple FY2023 10-K 0000320193-23-000106: opening equity $62,146M → $50,672M; 8 rows → 24 (GH #429) |
| `xbrl.current_period.cash_flow_statement()` on a 10-Q | the three-month duration | the year-to-date duration, as the 10-Q files it | Tesla Q2 2024 10-Q 0001628280-24-032662: 2 rows → 36 (GH #429) |
| `current_period` statements' `get_dataframe()` and `as_statement=False`: an instant balance row under a duration | left out | the balance the rendered table shows: the opening or closing instant | Apple FY2023 10-K 0000320193-23-000106: cash flow gains beginning cash $24,977M and ending cash $30,737M (GH #429) |
